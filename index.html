Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine 2026 Announced

Scientists Karl Deisseroth (US), Peter Hegemann, and Georg Nagel (Germany) were awarded the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine for their pioneering discoveries in optogenetics and light-gated ion channels. Their groundbreaking work allows scientists to use light to precisely control individual brain cells, fundamentally changing neuroscience research and potential treatments for neurological disorders.
